# Privacy Policy for Stash

**Last updated: June 3, 2026**

## Overview

Stash is a browser extension that helps you save and track products from around the web. This privacy policy explains how Stash handles your data.

## Data Collection

Stash does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All data you create while using Stash — including saved products, collections, notes, and price drop preferences — is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your own device.

No data is ever sent to any external server, third party, or the developer.

## What Stash Accesses

To function, Stash requests the following browser permissions:

- **Active tab** — to read product information (title, price, image) from the page you are currently viewing
- **Storage** — to save your stashed items locally on your device
- **Scripting** — to inject a content script that reads product data from web pages
- **Tabs** — to open the Stash gallery page

Stash reads page content solely to extract product details at the moment you choose to save an item. It does not track your browsing history or monitor pages you visit without interaction.

## Data Storage

All your saved items, collections, and notes are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's built-in storage. This data never leaves your device. If you uninstall the extension, all locally stored data is permanently deleted.

## Third-Party Services

Stash does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

Stash does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a future version, the updated policy will be included with the extension update and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
