Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

Stash is a Chrome extension that lets you save products and web pages while browsing. This policy explains how your data is handled.

Data we collect

Stash does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you save — including page titles, URLs, prices, images, notes, and collections — is stored exclusively in your browser using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.local API. This data never leaves your device.

Permissions used

  • storage — to save and retrieve your stashed items locally.
  • activeTab / tabs — to read the URL and title of the page you're currently viewing so it can be saved.
  • scripting — to inject the content script into pages that load after the extension is installed, so product data can be extracted.

No browsing history is recorded or transmitted.

Third-party services

Stash does not use any analytics, advertising, or third-party services of any kind.

Data deletion

You can delete all saved data at any time by removing the extension from Chrome, or by using the delete controls inside the extension. Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data.
